# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all participants in the NexusIntel community.

## Our Standards

We expect:
- Respectful and constructive communication
- Welcoming environment for all backgrounds
- Focus on ideas, not individuals
- Accountability for actions

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abuse
- Unwelcome comments on identity
- Personal attacks
- Threats or intimidation
- Deliberate disruption

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to xdrew87@github.com. All reports are reviewed confidentially.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
